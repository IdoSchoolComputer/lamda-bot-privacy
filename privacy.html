Lamda Bot: Privacy Policy

Lamda Bot is a personal, single-user assistant. It is used only by its owner.

What it accesses

With the owner's permission, the owner's own Google Calendar (events and free/busy times), only to read or change events at the owner's request.

What it stores

The sign-in token Google issues, kept on the owner's own server. Calendar contents are used to answer the owner's requests and are not copied to any other service, sold, shared or used for advertising.

Changes

The app only creates, edits or deletes events after the owner approves each change.

Revoking access

The owner can remove access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact

The developer contact email listed on the app's Google Cloud project.
